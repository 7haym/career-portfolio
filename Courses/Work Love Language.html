Workplace Action Guidelines

  • Words of Affirmation: Share specific praise and constructive feedback regularly.
  • Quality Time: Schedule uninterrupted 1-on-1 conversations and team check-ins.
  • New Opportunities & Challenges: Provide career growth initiatives and challenging tasks.
  • Acts of Service: Pitch in during peak workloads and share helpful resources.
  • Encouraging Touchpoints: Send thoughtful check-ins and ongoing positive notes.
