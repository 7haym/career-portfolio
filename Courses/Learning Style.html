Final takeaway

There is no single best way to learn or share

Understanding your preferences can help you make better choices about how you approach learning activities and communicate information.

Use visual materials when they help clarify information.
Listen, discuss, and explain ideas when verbal interaction is useful.
Practice and experiment when hands-on experience is helpful.
Be open to combining different approaches.

The goal is not to limit yourself to one learning preference. The goal is to become a more flexible and effective learner and knowledge sharer.
