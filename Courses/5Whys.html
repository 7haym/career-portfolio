Meet your investigation tool

What is the 5 Whys technique?

The 5 Whys is a simple problem-solving technique that helps you uncover the underlying cause of a problem by repeatedly asking one question:

WHY DID THIS HAPPEN?

Five is a guide, not a strict rule. Sometimes three Whys are enough; other times you need more. Keep going until you reach a cause you can address.

    Case File 002: Customer emails

    PROBLEM
    Customers are waiting too long for responses to their emails.
    1. WHY 1

      Why are customer emails not being answered on time?

      Because the team has more emails waiting for responses than it can process.

    2. WHY 2

      Why is there a growing backlog of emails?

      Because employees spend too much time sorting and assigning emails manually.

    3. WHY 3

      Why does sorting and assigning emails take so much time?

      Because there is no standard process for categorizing and assigning incoming emails.

    Let's recap the 5 Whys technique

    1. Identify the problem.
    2. Ask Why.
    3. Follow the chain of causes.
    4. Continue asking Why.
    5. Validate the root cause and address it.

    Remember these three things

    • Don't jump to conclusions.
    • Base your answers on facts and evidence.
    • Focus on fixing the process, not blaming people.
